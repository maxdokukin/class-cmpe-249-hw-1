# Suggested figure captions

Generated by hw1.figures; each caption carries the caveats that apply to its figure.

**F1_speed_accuracy.** Speed-accuracy trade-off. Sustained median latency per image at batch 1 (pre + forward + post) against mAP@[.5:.95], on COCO val2017 (left) and KITTI val-3769 (right). Grounding DINO-T was measured on the M4 CPU (an interrupted parity check), so its latency is not comparable to the MPS runs. OWLv2 and Grounding DINO-T COCO numbers are on a seeded 1000-image val2017 subset (the others use all 5000). KITTI mAP is COCO-style mAP@[.5:.95] over the 5 mapped classes (car, truck, pedestrian, cyclist, tram); COCO mAP is over 80 classes.

**F2_domain_shift.** Domain shift COCO val2017 to KITTI, per comparable class (car, truck, person/pedestrian) and the mean AP over those three. Lines connect the same model. OWLv2 and Grounding DINO-T COCO numbers are on a seeded 1000-image val2017 subset (the others use all 5000).

**F3_kitti_per_class.** KITTI per-class AP@[.5:.95] for every model with 95% bootstrap confidence intervals (500 resamples over images). Van counts as car; Cyclist is the person+bicycle union box (merge) for closed-set models. KITTI mAP is COCO-style mAP@[.5:.95] over the 5 mapped classes (car, truck, pedestrian, cyclist, tram); COCO mAP is over 80 classes.

**F4_scale.** AP by object size (small / medium / large, COCO area thresholds) on COCO (light) and KITTI (solid), per model. KITTI mAP is COCO-style mAP@[.5:.95] over the 5 mapped classes (car, truck, pedestrian, cyclist, tram); COCO mAP is over 80 classes. OWLv2 and Grounding DINO-T COCO numbers are on a seeded 1000-image val2017 subset (the others use all 5000).

**F5_ablations.** Ablations: change in mAP relative to each model's headline run. Cyclist naive (bicycle boxes only) and Cyclist dropped, Van as ignore region instead of car, and a 0.3 score cutoff (ngdet's default) on KITTI and on COCO. KITTI mAP is COCO-style mAP@[.5:.95] over the 5 mapped classes (car, truck, pedestrian, cyclist, tram); COCO mAP is over 80 classes. OWLv2 and Grounding DINO-T COCO numbers are on a seeded 1000-image val2017 subset (the others use all 5000).

**F6_difficulty.** KITTI-style AP by difficulty (easy, moderate, hard) for Car at IoU 0.7 and Pedestrian at IoU 0.5. KITTI-style AP uses COCO-style 101-point interpolation and is not comparable to the KITTI leaderboard.
